Day 2 · Produce → Reply

From Analysis to Reply

Turn yesterday's checked numbers into a one-page memo and a short deck, reply with the four checks, see an agent built — then complete one full request alone.

Mon 12 Oct 2026 09:00 – 16:00 CODED Campus, Kuwait

Workshop brief

Today covers the second half of the workflow. You will choose the right format for the reader, draft a memo in Word from your own checked numbers, build a deck in PowerPoint where every title is the message, and send a reply that passes four checks. After a short Agent Builder demo, you complete one full request alone — the graded project — then take the end MAP test and receive your certificate.
