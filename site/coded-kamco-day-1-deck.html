So what is AI?

Software that learns patterns from examples — and uses them to predict, sort, recommend or create — instead of only following rules a person wrote.

📈
Predict
What is likely next — a price move, a late payment, the next word.
🗂️
Classify
Which bucket — fraud or normal, urgent or not, equity or bond.
🎯
Recommend
What fits — a product, a film, a portfolio for a risk profile.
✍️
Create
New text, images or code — this is generative AI.
Not magicNot always rightNot a decision-maker

💳

🏦

📊
Robo-advisers & trading

🗺️

📧

None of these write anything. They predict, classify or recommend. Writing is one kind of AI — the newest.
Where LLMs fit

Every LLM is AI. Not every AI is an LLM.

Artificial intelligencethe whole field — machines doing tasks that need human-like judgement
Machine learninglearns from data instead of fixed rules — e.g. fraud detection
Deep learninglarge neural networks — e.g. face recognition
Generative AIcreates new content — text, images, audio
Large language modelsgenerative AI for language — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, the engine in Copilot
An LLM is a specialist inside a big family. Your bank's fraud model is AI — but it cannot write an email.
The kind behind Copilot

And what is an LLM?

Large Language Model — a pattern machine trained on huge amounts of text. It writes by predicting the next piece of text.

📖
Read almost everything
Books, websites, code — billions of pages of text.
🎯
Predicts the next piece
It does not look facts up. It continues the pattern.
🧊
Frozen at training
It knows nothing after its training date — and nothing about your clients.
Examples: GPT (OpenAI · ChatGPT) · Claude (Anthropic) · Gemini (Google) · Llama (Meta). Microsoft 365 Copilot is built on models like these.

The recipe

CTFT — four ingredients.

C
Context
The situation — who you are, who it is for.
T
Task
What to do — one strong verb.
F
Format
The shape — length, bullets, table.
T
Tone
The voice — formal, warm, plain.
A weak answer = a missing ingredient.
CTFT · 1 of 4 · Context
C

Context — the setup.

Copilot knows the world — not your situation.

WeakSummarise this portfolio.
StrongI am the relationship manager for Al-Waha Family Office. The family meets us this week and is worried about property.
Answer:Who am I?What is the situation?Who is it for?Any limits?
CTFT · 2 of 4 · Task
T

Task — the what.

Name the action.

WeakHelp me with this file.
StrongUsing /awfo-q3-custody-export.xlsx, check real estate against the mandate and list any breach.
Strong verbs:SummariseCompareCheckDraftRewriteListExplain
One main task per prompt.
CTFT · 3 of 4 · Format
F

Format — the shape.

Say nothing, and Copilot picks for you.

WeakWrite something for Reem.
StrongFive bullet points with KWD figures, then one sentence on next steps. Under 120 words.
Specify:LengthBullets or tableDocument typeUnits (KWD, %)
CTFT · 4 of 4 · Tone
T

Tone — the voice.

Same facts, different feel.

WeakWrite an email.
StrongCalm and reassuring, but honest about any breach. Plain English for a family CFO.
Try:FormalWarmConciseFirmPlain English
