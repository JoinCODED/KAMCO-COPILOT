Scheduled Prompts · How

Four steps. Up to ten schedules.

  1. Run the prompt once in Copilot Chat — microsoft365.com/chat, Teams or Outlook.Make sure the answer is what you want first.
  2. Hover over your prompt → Schedule this prompt.
  3. Choose when it runs, how many times, and turn on the email notification. Save.
  4. Results appear in your chat list on the left.Manage them: … → Scheduled prompts → Run now · Turn off · Delete.

Good to schedule: a repeat question about new information. Not a one-off.

Checking The Work · Before You Hit Send

Four checks, every time.

Accuracy
Every number traced back to Excel. Every claim to its source.
Confidentiality
No other client's data. Compliance copied if it goes to a client.
Tone
Right for the reader. No promises of returns. Coaching by Copilot.
Completeness
Every ask in the newest email answered. Both files attached.

You sign it. You own it.
