Trainee lab — solutions

7 challenges
Flag format KAMCO{ } — tap a flag to copy.
01 · Safe use

Safe or Not?

FLAGKAMCO{share_only_what_the_task_needs}
Fine the custody export and the house view — your client, your task, work account. After removing personal data the complaint email — the summary does not need the civil ID or phone number. Never your password, and client data in a public chatbot.
02 · Prompting

Build the Brief

FLAGKAMCO{context_task_format_tone}
Context: who you are + the CFO meeting. Task: calculate value and Q3 change; flag mandate breaches (two task pieces). Format: a two-column table in KWD. Tone: plain English for a family CFO.
03 · Outlook

Summarise the Thread

FLAGKAMCO{check_the_summary_against_the_thread}
Run Summarise on the thread in Outlook and read the newest message yourself. Wrong “due Wednesday 14 Oct” — Faisal moved it to Tuesday 13 Oct, 3:00 pm. Wrong “use the June factsheet” — Faisal said the opposite: use the 30 Sep custody export. Wrong “send the Al-Rumaithiya report” — another client's data never leaves. Decline politely. Correct the deliverables, the new meeting time, Khalid's two questions, and copying Noura.
04 · Outlook

Read the Request

FLAGKAMCO{the_newest_message_wins}
Run Summarise on the thread, then the checklist prompt. Deadline: Tue 13 Oct, 3:00 pm (newest message). Meeting: Wed 14 Oct, 11:00. Deliverables: 5-slide client deck + one-page memo. Copy: Noura in Compliance. Decline: the Al-Rumaithiya family report — confidential to another client.
05 · Excel

Clean the Export

FLAGKAMCO{clean_before_you_count}
Raw export: 26 rows. Duplicates by Holding ID: 4 (Atlas Tech, Gulf Trade Bank, US Treasury 2030, Failaka Foods). After removal: 22 holdings. Missing prices: NWH and GHR — filled from Price_List.
06 · Excel

Value and Mandate

FLAGKAMCO{check_the_mandate}
Total value at 30 Sep: KWD 4,696,424 (with duplicates it would be KWD 5,785,022 — wrong). Q3 change: KWD 112,674 = 2.46%. Allocation: Equity 61.0%, Fixed Income 22.8%, Real Estate 8.3%, Cash 7.9%. Breach: Equity (max 55%).
07 · Excel

Issuers and Movers

FLAGKAMCO{the_whole_issuer_counts}
Al-Noor Bank shares + sukuk = 12.21% → above the 10% limit. Top 3: Al-Noor Bank (+38,640), Atlas Tech Corp (+32,955), Harbor Semiconductors (+31,447). Bottom 3: Sadaf Logistics (-24,300), Gulf Horizon REIT (-10,360), Northwind Health (-7,552).
