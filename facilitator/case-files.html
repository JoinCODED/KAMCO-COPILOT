3 · How to set up the Excel file

  1. Save amfo-q3-custody-export.xlsx to your OneDrive and open it in Excel. Turn AutoSave on.
  2. Delete rows 1–3 above the headers. Click inside the data and press Ctrl+T to make it a table.
  3. Open Copilot in Excel and follow the prompts from the deck. Check the row count after every step.
  4. Keep the file. You need it on Day 2 for the memo, the deck and the reply.
