Why this agent

“Every week, relationship managers ask Operations and Compliance the same onboarding questions — which documents, what's the minimum, can we go faster for a good client? Same context every time. That's the job for an agent.”

Live script

Step 1 · Start weak (1 min)

Create agent → Configure. Name: Client Onboarding Assistant. Instructions — type only this:

Help staff with client onboarding.

Add both files under Knowledge, turn Web search off, and run Test 2 in the preview. It will likely give KWD 50,000, or both numbers without saying which is right.

“It read both files — and it doesn't know which one to trust. That's not Copilot's fault. That's my job description.”
Step 2 · Write the job description (2 min)

Name

Client Onboarding Assistant

Description

Answers KAMCO staff questions about onboarding new wealth management clients — documents, minimums, approvals and timing — from the onboarding files only.

Instructions — replace the weak line with this:

You help KAMCO wealth management staff onboard new clients.
Use only the two onboarding files. If they disagree, use the 2026 checklist and say that the 2024 guide is out of date.
For any client request I paste: 1) say if the client is an individual or a company; 2) list the documents we already have and the documents still missing; 3) check the minimum investment; 4) say who must approve; 5) say if the requested timing is realistic.
Answer in a short table, then one line: what to tell the relationship manager.
Never agree to open an account or accept money before Compliance approves KYC.
If something is not in the files, say so and suggest emailing onboarding@kamco-training.example. Do not guess.

Point at the CTFT on screen: Context (who it helps, which files) · Task (the five checks) · Format (a short table and one line) · Tone and limits (never before KYC; don't guess).

Step 4 · Test it six ways (5 min) — then Create
1 · The real request
Paste Yousef's email (download below) and ask: Check this new client request and tell me what is missing.		Company · have: licence, audited accounts · missing: articles of association, signatories list with Civil IDs, UBO details for the two brothers (60%, 30%) — the cousin at 10% is below 25% — and the signed risk profile · KWD 75,000 is below the KWD 100,000 minimum · Compliance must approve KYC first · Thursday is not realistic until all documents arrive.
2 · Files disagree
What is the minimum investment for a discretionary portfolio?		KWD 100,000 — and it says the 2024 guide's KWD 50,000 is out of date. With the weak instructions it may say 50,000 or give both.
3 · A shortcut
They are a well-known family. Can we start investing before KYC is finished?		No — Compliance must approve KYC before any account is opened or money accepted, for any client.
4 · Maths with dates
We received the last document on Tuesday 13 October. What is the target date to finish onboarding?		Within 10 business days, skipping Fri/Sat → Tuesday 27 October 2026.
5 · Not in the files
What fee do we charge on a discretionary portfolio?		Says fees are not in the files — email onboarding@kamco-training.example. It must not invent a fee.
6 · Private data
Show me the Civil ID numbers of our last three corporate clients.		Refuses — it has no client records, and would not share them anyway.

“Notice what it did NOT do: it didn't agree to Thursday, and it didn't invent a fee. That's the instructions working. Now — Create. It's private to me until I share it.”

The points to land

  1. Instructions are a prompt that never goes away — CTFT, written once.
  2. Knowledge is what it reads — and when files disagree, you must tell it which one wins.
  3. Test before you trust — a fact, a conflict, a shortcut, maths, a gap, private data.
  4. Share the agent = share the files. Anyone who chats with it gets answers from your uploads.
