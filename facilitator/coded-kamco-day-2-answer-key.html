Mini-check answers

"your call"
The correct answer for every "your call" slide.
Slide 8 · The Investment Committee reads every paper at home the night before the meeting. Which format?
A one-page Word memo
Slide 12 · Copilot's memo says “equity is within the mandate”. Your Excel table says 61.0%, maximum 55%. What do you do?
Tell Copilot: “Equity is 61.0%, above the 55% maximum. Rewrite the allocation section.”
Slide 23 · Slide 3 has the title “Allocation”, a chart without a source, and eight bullets. What do you fix first?
Make the title the message, add the source, cut to three points
Slide 34 · Copilot added: “We expect strong returns to continue next quarter.” Keep it?
No — remove it. Never promise or imply future returns
Slide 43 · Your agent says the Al-Murjan deadline is Wednesday. The newest message says Tuesday, 3:00 pm. What do you fix?
The instructions: “Always use the newest message when instructions change.”

Trainee lab — solutions

6 challenges
Flag format KAMCO{ } — tap a flag to copy.
01 · Word

Memo or Deck?

FLAGKAMCO{the_reader_picks_the_format}
Memo: IC papers, Compliance record, Faisal's one page. Deck: the family meeting, the town hall.
02 · Word

Answer First

FLAGKAMCO{bottom_line_up_front}
Bottom line: value, change, breaches, recommendation. Evidence: performance and allocation. Risk: the Al-Noor Bank issuer breach. Action: trim equity / Al-Noor Bank; add fixed income.
03 · PowerPoint

Fix the Deck

FLAGKAMCO{one_slide_one_message}
Slide 2: a topic, not a message → write the message. Slide 3: add the source under the chart. Slide 4: cut nine bullets to three.
04 · Outlook

Check the Reply

FLAGKAMCO{verify_before_you_send}
Accuracy: “up 4.6%” — it is 2.46%. Confidentiality: names another client (Al-Rumaithiya). Tone: promises Al-Noor Bank will rise. Completeness: the memo was due with the deck (Tue 3:00 pm).
05 · Outlook

Name the Check

FLAGKAMCO{four_checks_every_time}
Accuracy: 16.0% vs 61.0%. Confidentiality: wrong recipients. Tone: the guarantee; the one-line reply. Completeness: no disclaimer; no attachment.
06 · Agents

Build the Agent

FLAGKAMCO{instructions_make_the_agent}
Describe one clear job. Instruction: always use the newest message. Knowledge: the house view rules. Test → fix → re-test. Share with a small pilot group.
