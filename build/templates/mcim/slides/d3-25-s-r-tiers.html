Compliance · The Four Tiers

Same company, four kinds of data. Know the difference.

Public
Anyone can see it
  • Menu and prices on the website
  • A press release
  • A job advert, once posted
In CopilotYes — as it is
Internal
All staff
  • Team meeting notes
  • HR policies, the staff handbook
  • The café rota
In CopilotYes — work account only
Confidential
Only people who need it
  • Results before they are announced
  • A client proposal with prices
  • A supplier contract or dispute
In CopilotWork account only — remove what you don't need
Restricted
A named few · protected by law
  • Civil IDs, phone and card numbers
  • Salaries, reviews, medical records
  • Passwords and API keys
In CopilotRemove every identifier — or never

The test: if this leaked, who gets hurt — and how badly?
