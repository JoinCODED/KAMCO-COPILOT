Weak

Block 3 · Safety with AI

The fastest way to lose the tool is to misuse it once. One client's data in the wrong box. One answer sent unchecked. Then everything is under review.

Right data. Inside the walls. A person accountable.

Security · 1 of 5 — Where Your Data Goes

Protected at work. Not used to train the model.

🏛️
Enterprise data protection
Same protection as your work email and files.
🚫
No training on your data
Your prompts and files don't train the models.
🌐
Web search is different
Only a short query goes to Bing — not your file.
Security · 2 of 5 — Just-Enough Access

Copilot sees what you see — no more, no less.

The bigger risk isn't data leaking out. It's data over-shared in.

🔑
It uses your permissions
Only what your account can open.
🔍
Search on steroids
Access to a salary file by mistake? Copilot finds it.
🧹
Clean up your sharing
Fix old “anyone with the link” shares.
Security · 3 of 5 — Safe Input

Strip it before you paste.

🛑
The golden rule
No Civil IDs, phones, cards, passwords, HR or medical data.
🎭
Use placeholders
“Khalid Al-Mutairi” → “Customer A”. Same answer.
🏷️
Labels do work
“Confidential” labels can block Copilot. Respect them.
Security · 4 of 5 — Human In The Loop

The AI is an intern. You stay accountable.

The danger: trusting it because it sounds right.

✔️
Check before it leaves your hands
You sign it, you own it — not Copilot.
⚠️
Know the blind spots
Maths, recent events, local detail, PDF tables.
Security · 5 of 5 — Ethics & Bias

It drafts. It never decides.

⚖️
No decisions about people
Hiring, pay, credit: a person decides.
👁️
Watch the language
“A young, energetic man” → “a supervisor who can lead a busy shift”.
📝
Say when AI helped
Say when Copilot did much of the work.

Compliance

Whose rules apply? Three layers.

  1. Your company's policy — first.It sorts data into four tiers. Next slide.
  2. Your sector's regulator.e.g. Central Bank of Kuwait · CITRA's Data Privacy Protection Regulation (2024)
  3. Microsoft's controls in your tenant.Labels, audit, retention — set by IT.

Not sure? Don't paste. Ask.

Compliance · The Four Tiers

Same company, four kinds of data. Know the difference.

Public
Anyone can see it
  • Menu and prices on the website
  • A press release
  • A job advert, once posted
In CopilotYes — as it is
Internal
All staff
  • Team meeting notes
  • HR policies, the staff handbook
  • The café rota
In CopilotYes — work account only
Confidential
Only people who need it
  • Results before they are announced
  • A client proposal with prices
  • A supplier contract or dispute
In CopilotWork account only — remove what you don't need
Restricted
A named few · protected by law
  • Civil IDs, phone and card numbers
  • Salaries, reviews, medical records
  • Passwords and API keys
In CopilotRemove every identifier — or never

The test: if this leaked, who gets hurt — and how badly?
