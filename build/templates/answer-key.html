Topic 3 · Policy

Find the gaps

A real-looking access-control policy — 5 dangerous clauses
→
5 gaps to tap:
  1. Shared admin account (4.1) — no individual accountability.
  2. Access on email request, same day (5.0) — no approval/verification.
  3. Leaver access reviewed once a year (6.0) — stale access lingers.
  4. Exceptions approved verbally (7.0) — no audit trail.
  5. 30-day forced rotation, only "previous" reuse barred (3.2) — outdated practice.
Reading a policy critically is the skill.

Mini-check answers

"your call"
The multiple-choice "your call" prompts — the correct answer for each.
T1 · Which takes a brute-force attack longer to crack?
A 16-character phrase of plain words
T1 · Why try "Summer2023!" before "x9#mK2vQ"?
It's a common pattern — top of the wordlist
T1 · Reuse one strong password; a shop is breached — bank safe?
No — the leaked password gets tried at the bank
T2 · "ISO 27001 certified" — what does it tell you?
It runs an audited security management system
T2 · The five NIST CSF functions?
Identify · Protect · Detect · Respond · Recover
T3 · Which of these is a policy statement?
"Access to customer data must be restricted to authorised staff."
T3 · "Passwords must be strong" — what makes it enforceable?
A standard: ≥14 chars, MFA required, no reuse
T3 · Who most needs the procedure written down?
The person performing the task

Trainee lab — solutions

5 challenges
Each challenge, its flag, and the path to it. Flag format KIBS{ } — tap a flag to copy.
01 · Hashing

Crack the Hash

FLAGKIBS{length_beats_complexity}
Launch the dictionary attack: password123, Summer2024!, qwerty fall in milliseconds. The 28-char passphrase correct horse battery staple survives — it's not in any wordlist. Length, not symbols.
02 · Credential stuffing

The Domino

FLAGKIBS{reuse_is_a_skeleton_key}
At-risk = reuses the breached password AND has no MFA: Personal email, Streaming, Social media. Safe: the bank (reuse but MFA on) and work SSO (unique password).
03 · Standards mapping

Map the Control

FLAGKIBS{one_control_many_frameworks}
Encrypt cardholder data → PCI; audited ISMS → ISO; Identify·Protect·Detect·Respond·Recover → NIST; MFA on admin and access logs → all three.
04 · Policy gap

Mind the Gap

FLAGKIBS{a_policy_without_a_procedure}
Sort policy / standard / procedure — then mark the gap: decommissioned servers recycled with no documented wipe procedure. The Morgan Stanley failure: the policy existed; the procedure never ran.
05 · Incident response

The 02:00 Call

FLAGKIBS{contain_before_you_clean}
Detect (confirm scope in SIEM, no reboot) → Contain (isolate, don't power off) → Eradicate (patch the entry before reconnecting) → Recover (clean offline backups, verify) → Learn (post-incident review). Contain before you clean.
